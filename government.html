Your role is public record. Your home address shouldn't be.

Directories, filings and coverage make people in public service easy to look up. Data brokers do the rest, linking a name to an address, a phone number and a family.

  1. Harassment at home

    A listed home address turns a public disagreement into a visit to someone's front door.

  2. Targeting through family

    Spouses' and children's names, ages and addresses are often on the same profile.

  3. Impersonation and social engineering

    Personal details make fake calls and messages convincing enough to get past verification.
