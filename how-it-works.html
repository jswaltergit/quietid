How it works

How QuietID works.

We find your listings, file the removals, and keep checking. You see every step on your dashboard.

Start free scan

Five steps, then we keep going.

  1. Scan

    We search 000+ data broker and people-search sites for your name, addresses, phone numbers and email.

    What we look forCurrent and past addresses, phone numbers, email, relatives, age and employer.

  2. Confirm

    We match each listing to you, so we only remove what's yours. You can review matches on your dashboard.

    Why it mattersCommon names turn up many people. Matching keeps your requests accurate.

  3. Remove

    We file opt-out requests with every site that lists you and follow up until each one is done.

    How long it takesSome sites act in days; others take weeks. Each request shows its status.

  4. Monitor

    Brokers refresh their data and relist people. We re-scan on a set schedule and file again when your details reappear.

    No extra costRe-removals are included in every plan.

  5. Report

    Your dashboard shows what was found, what came down and what's in progress, with a Risk Score that drops as removals land.

    AlertsWe tell you when your details show up somewhere new.

What to expect.

  1. First day

    Your scan results and first removal requests. Confirm

  2. First weeks

    Most removals complete and your Risk Score starts to drop. Confirm

  3. Ongoing

    Regular re-scans, and new requests whenever your data comes back.

What we can and can't remove.

We're clear about the limits, so you know what to expect.

We remove

  • Listings on data broker and people-search sites
  • Background-check style profiles
  • Marketing and lead-generation databases confirm

We can't remove

  • Government records at the source, such as property and court records
  • News articles and other published reporting
  • Content you post yourself on social media
