What is a data broker?

A company that collects personal information from public records, apps, retailers and other sources, packages it into profiles and sells access. People-search sites are the most visible kind: anyone can look you up by name.

What do QuietID's analysts do?

They review what our technology finds, confirm which listings are yours, follow up with sites that are slow or don't comply, and help you understand new exposures when they appear. To confirm

What happens when you find something new?

We file a removal right away and tell you in your dashboard. If it's something we can't remove directly, such as a dark-web exposure, an analyst explains what it means and the steps to take. Process to confirm

Who can see my information?

Only U.S.-based personnel, and only to find and remove your listings. We never sell your data. Wording to confirm

Can you cover my family?

Yes. Family members often appear on the same listings, so plans can cover your household, each person with their own dashboard. Confirm for government plans

How long does removal take?

It depends on the site. Some remove a listing within days; others take weeks. Your dashboard shows the status of every request. Typical time to first removals: to confirm.
