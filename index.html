What is a data broker?

A company that collects personal information from public records, apps, retailers and other sources, packages it into profiles and sells access. People-search sites are the most visible kind: anyone can look you up by name.

How long does removal take?

It depends on the site. Some remove a listing within days; others take weeks. Your dashboard shows the status of every request, so you always know where things stand. Typical time to first removals: [to confirm].

Will my information come back?

Often, yes. Brokers refresh their data and relist people. That's why QuietID keeps scanning after the first removals and files new requests when your data reappears.

What do you need from me, and what do you store?

Enough to find and verify your listings: your name, current and past addresses, phone numbers and email. We use it to file removals on your behalf. We never sell your data.

Can I cover my family?

Yes. Family plans cover up to 5 people, each with their own dashboard and results.
